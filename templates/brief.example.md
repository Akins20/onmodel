# <Product name> art brief

## Product
What the product is, in two sentences, and what this art is for: an app icon, a game's characters, onboarding illustrations, a sticker pack, a hero image.

## Audience
Who sees it, on what screens and at what sizes, and what they compare it with.

## Art direction
The style in words a painter could follow: flat or shaded, line weight, lighting, mood, era, level of detail. Name two or three existing works it should sit beside.

## Palette
The colours the art must use, as hex values in order of importance, with what each is for. If the product has design tokens, name the file in context.files instead and say which tokens are the brand.

## References
What each file in `references` is and what to take from it: the logo, an existing character, a style sheet. The model is shown the files; this tells it how to read them.

## Do not
Styles, subjects, text, watermarks, gradients, or anything else that must not appear.
