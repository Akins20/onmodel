# Settled decisions

One bullet per decision about the art that is made and not up for review. Give the
reason after a colon; the judge reads this list and does not mark a candidate down
for honouring one of them, and the prompts carry them so the model stops proposing
otherwise. Keep it short and honest: a decision here is closed for every run.

- Example: the cat has no collar: it is a stray, and that is the story.
- Example: outlines stay black at two pixels: the whole set was drawn that way.
